## 1. VOT 11000 — Salary and Wages — **RM3,000.00**

*Paste into the VOT 11000 box:*

Undergraduate research assistant engaged directly on field sampling, plate inoculation and
monitoring device assembly.

Rate: RM7.00 per hour, maximum 4 hours per day = RM28.00 per day.
Monthly engagement: approximately 18 working days = **RM500.00 per month**.
Active months: 3 months per year, timed to the sampling rounds and device build.

- Year 1: RM500.00 x 3 months = **RM1,500.00**
- Year 2: RM500.00 x 3 months = **RM1,500.00**
- **Total: RM3,000.00** (at the RM3,000 ceiling for temporary and contract personnel)

Justification. Six quarterly sampling rounds each require swabbing 36 fixed points across four
buildings and four facade orientations, on-site inoculation of SDA and PDA plates, covariate and
algal cover recording, and cold-chain packing for transfer to SRDC-TROPI — approximately 2 full
field days plus 1 preparation and packing day per round. The assistant additionally supports
assembly, enclosure fitting and field installation of the air-quality monitoring nodes in Year 1.
Engagement is concentrated into the active months rather than spread across the year, so the
allocation buys approximately 107 assistant-days of direct project labour. The appointment also
delivers the human capital development outcome stated in the Expected Result.

## 2. VOT 21000 — Travelling and Transportation — **RM3,000.00**

*Paste into the VOT 21000 box:*

**(a) Cold-chain transport of biological materials, Sibu to Kota Samarahan — RM1,350.00**
SRDC-TROPI supplies prepared sterile swabs and media to UTS, and UTS returns inoculated plates
and swabs for incubation. Media are batched to cover two rounds per inbound consignment, while
returns must follow each round individually because the cultures are live.
3 inbound consignments + 6 return consignments = 9 legs x RM150.00 = **RM1,350.00**
Materials are temperature-sensitive live cultures requiring sealed triple packaging under
cold-chain conditions per MoA Clause 3.3(b); ordinary post is not usable.

Inbound delivery of sensor modules, enclosures and field consumables to UTS Sibu.
RM50.00 per delivery x 3 deliveries = **RM150.00**

Travel and subsistence for one researcher to present project findings at an indexed conference,
in support of the two indexed outputs the guideline requires.

## 3. VOT 24000 — Rental — **RM1,200.00**

*Paste into the VOT 24000 box:*

Rental of a reference-grade air quality analyser for co-location calibration of the low-cost
monitoring nodes under Sibu field conditions.

RM600.00 per week x 2 weeks = **RM1,200.00**
- Campaign 1, Year 1: pre-deployment calibration of all nodes against the reference instrument.
- Campaign 2, Year 1: post-installation verification after field mounting.

Justification. Low-cost optical particulate sensors are subject to hygroscopic bias at the high
relative humidity that prevails on this campus, and electrochemical gas sensors drift. Co-located
reference measurement is what allows a correction to be derived and the reported values to be
defensible rather than indicative only. Rental rather than purchase is appropriate because the
instrument is required for two short campaigns, not continuously. Rental is placed in VOT 24000
rather than VOT 35000 in line with the guideline treatment of rental.

## 5. VOT 29000 — Professional Services — **RM3,000.00**

*Paste into the VOT 29000 box:*

**(a) Sanger sequencing of ITS amplicons — RM2,000.00**
80 reactions x RM25.00 per reaction = **RM2,000.00**
Quoted rate, NextGene Scientific Sdn Bhd, quotation QT2610/0124, inclusive of sample QC by gel
electrophoresis and one free re-run where a result is unsatisfactory.
Sample testing and analysis: SRDC-TROPI performs DNA extraction and PCR amplification of the
internal transcribed spacer region from the curated isolate set, and the amplicons are forwarded
to the provider for sequencing. The 80 reactions cover a curated set of up to 60 isolates
spanning the morphotype range across four buildings, three facade materials and six sampling
rounds, by single-pass read, with bidirectional reads on the subset whose assignment is
ambiguous.

**(b) SORAS application and SBC permit fees — RM500.00**
Fees for the Sarawak Online Research Application System submission and for the Sarawak
Biodiversity Council permit covering this project, its scope, its personnel and the
participating institutions, together with any renewal falling within the project term. Research
may not be conducted in Sarawak without a valid permit, so this is a precondition of all
fieldwork rather than an optional cost.

**(c) Conference registration fee — RM500.00**
Registration for one indexed conference in Year 2, supporting the guideline requirement of two
indexed conference or journal outputs. Associated travel, accommodation and subsistence are
claimed under VOT 21000, giving a total conference provision of RM2,000.00 across the two vots.

- Year 1: RM800.00 sequencing + RM500.00 SORAS and permit fees = **RM1,300.00**
- Year 2: RM1,200.00 sequencing + RM500.00 registration = **RM1,700.00**
- **Total: RM3,000.00** (at the RM3,000 ceiling for Science, Engineering and Technology)

Note. Journal article processing charges are **not** requested, as the ceiling is committed to
sequencing, permits and conference registration. Non-APC or society journals are therefore
targeted for both indexed outputs.
